## 1. Register Architecture & Confidence Matrix

Every statutory fact or procedural requirement presented in PassportReady is backed by an audited official record.

### Confidence Levels:
- **`DIRECT`**: Official primary authority or legislation explicitly states the exact fee, rule, timeframe, or document requirement.
- **`PARTIAL`**: Primary authority gives general procedural requirements, but specific mission tariffs or processing timeframes depend on local mission discretion.
- **`AMBIGUOUS`**: Primary source contains outdated or conflicting circulars; flagged for human/consular clarification.
- **`UNAVAILABLE`**: No authoritative public documentation currently exists online; kept strictly at `general_guidance` or `awaiting_research`.

## 3. Statutory Exception & Integrity Summary

All statutory exceptions have been independently reviewed and validated:
1. **Malaysia (`MY`)**: JIM Staff replacement fee tiers (RM 200 1st loss / RM 500 2nd loss / RM 1,000 3rd loss) strictly apply only to 10-year validity booklets. 5-year replacement is codified as `not_eligible`. Original issuance is RM 0.
2. **Indonesia (`ID`)**: Under-17 legal marriage exception (Permenkumham No. 18/2022 Pasal 2A) is preserved with `confirmation_required` fee status and explicit verification flags when civil marital status is unconfirmed.
3. **Mexico (`MX`)**: Emergency appointment surcharge of 30% under Art. 20 of Ley Federal de Derechos (LFD) and Type E emergency passport validity ceilings (maximum 1 year) are preserved.
4. **New Zealand (`NZ`)**: Te Tari Taiwhenua (DIA) statutory callout fee breakdown (Adult total NZD 1,001 = NZD 428 urgent + NZD 573 callout; Child total NZD 898 = NZD 342 urgent + NZD 556 callout) is verified.
