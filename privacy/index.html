Privacy by Design · Zero Server Retention

Privacy Policy

PassportReady is built on an absolute privacy-first foundation. We do not store personal data, we do not require document uploads, and we do not use tracking cookies.

Effective Date: October 2026
Applicable Frameworks: General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), UK-GDPR, California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India DPDP Act).

1. The Foundation: Zero Server-Side Identity Storage

Unlike commercial passport preparation agencies or document expediting platforms, PassportReady operates with zero server-side data retention:

  • We never collect or ask for your passport number: No national ID numbers (e.g. SSN, Aadhaar, MyKad, NIN), no passport booklet numbers, and no biometric credentials.
  • Zero Document Uploads: You are never asked to upload scans, PDFs, or photos of your government credentials. All question flows evaluate eligibility based on non-sensitive question choices.
  • 100% Local In-Browser Processing: The readiness engine, photo aspect-ratio checker, and name discrepancy matching execute entirely on your device inside your web browser. No payload containing your inputs is transmitted across the network.

2. What Is Stored Locally on Your Device (LocalStorage)

If you click the optional "Save Progress" button on the readiness checker, the application saves your non-sensitive wizard selections directly into your browser's local sandbox (localStorage). Specifically, this includes:

Contents of Stored Progress

  • The chosen country code (e.g. "IN" or "US").
  • Selected application pathway (e.g. "renewal_reissue", "adult").
  • Selected processing speed (e.g. "standard" or "urgent_expedited").
  • Boolean checkmarks for completed document checklist items (e.g. "proof_id_checked: true").

How to delete stored data: You can permanently wipe this data from your browser at any time by clicking "Start New Check" or "Clear My Data" on the checker card, or by clearing your browser cache/cookies.

3. Share Link Encoding

The "Share Checklist" button creates a URL containing hash parameters (for example, #country=IN&service=renewal_reissue&age=adult). This link encodes only high-level questionnaire parameters so you can bookmark your progress or share a generic checklist with a family member. The URL hash never contains names, dates of birth, identity numbers, or personal identifying information.

4. Cookies & Analytics

PassportReady utilizes zero third-party tracking cookies, zero advertising pixels, and zero fingerprinting technologies. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with data brokers or advertising networks.

5. External Government Links

PassportReady provides outbound direct hyperlinks to official government portals (such as passportindia.gov.in, travel.state.gov, gov.uk). Once you click an external link to visit a sovereign government website, their respective privacy policies and terms govern your interaction.

6. Data Controller & Inquiries

Because PassportReady does not collect or process personal data on servers, we do not maintain centralized databases of citizen records. However, for any privacy questions or rights inquiries under GDPR, UK-GDPR, CCPA, or DPDP:

  • Privacy Officer: MyOSS Civic Technology Initiative
  • Email: privacy@myoss.in or contact@myoss.in
  • Project Repository: https://github.com/Asadxio/passportready-global
