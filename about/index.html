About PassportReady

PassportReady is an independent, non-governmental preparation engine designed to help citizens, expatriates, and traveling families navigate passport rules with precision and complete data privacy.

Strict Independence Statement: PassportReady is not an official government agency and is not affiliated with any foreign ministry, immigration department, embassy, or consulate. We do not issue passports, schedule official appointments, or accept passport applications. All official submissions must occur on designated government portals.

Why PassportReady Exists

Applying for or renewing a passport is one of the most consequential administrative interactions a citizen has with their government. Yet millions of applications face delays, administrative rejections, or forfeiture of fees each year due to simple, preventable issues:

  • Clerical Mismatches: Transliteration differences, missing middle names, or inverted given/family names between national identity cards and birth certificates.
  • Biometric Photo Rejections: Millimeter deviations in chin-to-crown ratio, improper white/cream/blue background shades, or glare on prescription eyewear.
  • Complex Fee Structures: Navigating domestic vs. consular surcharges, urgent Tatkaal/Expedited fees, minor validity scales, and booklet size differentials.
  • International Validity Traps: Traveling without realizing a destination enforces the strict 6-month validity rule or Schengen's 10-year issuance requirement.

PassportReady was built to solve this problem before citizens stand in consular queues or pay non-refundable processing fees.

Our Core Architecture Promises

  • Zero Server Data: 100% of the checklist logic, name discrepancy analysis, and photo cropping runs client-side inside your browser. No personal identifiers or document scans are ever transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • Zero Trackers: No advertising networks, behavioral profiling trackers, or invasive analytics cookies.
  • Official Sources Only: Every rule, document checklist, fee figure, and statutory link derives strictly from primary government publications (e.g. MEA India, US Department of State, UK HM Passport Office, Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia) and international standards (ICAO Doc 9303).
  • Transparent Limitations: When a country's rules have not yet undergone full statutory verification, we explicitly designate them as "Awaiting Research" with generic baseline guidance rather than guessing.

Who Builds This

PassportReady is maintained as an open-source public service under the MyOSS civic tech initiative by independent software engineers, accessibility advocates, and international open-data contributors. The project source code is publicly accessible on GitHub.
