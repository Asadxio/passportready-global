Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Check eligibility and apply in person at any Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia (JIM) passport office or Urban Transformation Centre (UTC). [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Present original MyKad (identity smartcard) for biometric verification. [Official Source ↗]
  3. Step 3: Choose validity period (5 years or 10 years, if eligible) and pay official statutory fee by debit/credit card. [Official Source ↗]
  4. Step 4: Capture live biometric facial photo and thumbprints at the counter; collect finished passport typically within 1 hour after payment. [Official Source ↗]

Required Documents Checklist

Gather all original records and photocopies before visiting an acceptance facility or consular mission.

MyKad (National Identity Card) REQUIRED

Original MyKad in good condition with functional chip. For children under 12, MyKid and original Birth Certificate required.

Kad Pendaftaran OKU (if claiming disability exemption) CONDITIONAL

Original OKU card issued by Jabatan Kebajikan Masyarakat (JKM).

Official Immigration Department / Ministry Service Credentials (for official exemptions) CONDITIONAL

Official Immigration Department service ID for JIM staff; official Kementerian Luar Negeri (KLN) authorization letter for PMD/PMR; formal principal dependency confirmation for PMA dependents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Fresh Passport in Malaysia?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia (JIM) via their official portal at https://www.imi.gov.my/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Malaysia Fresh Passport?

Primary requirements include: MyKad (National Identity Card), Kad Pendaftaran OKU (if claiming disability exemption), Official Immigration Department / Ministry Service Credentials (for official exemptions), plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
