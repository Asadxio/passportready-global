Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Both parents present valid official IDs, child's birth certificate, and sign Form OP-7 in front of SRE consular officer. [Official Source ↗]

Required Documents Checklist

Gather all original records and photocopies before visiting an acceptance facility or consular mission.

Form OP-7 Parental Consent & Parents' Photo IDs REQUIRED

If one parent resides in another state or abroad, Form OP-7/II must be executed at another SRE delegation or consulate prior to the appointment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Minor & Child Passport in Mexico?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Secretaría de Relaciones Exteriores (SRE - Ministry of Foreign Affairs) via their official portal at https://www.gob.mx/pasaporte or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Mexico Minor & Child Passport?

Primary requirements include: Form OP-7 Parental Consent & Parents' Photo IDs, plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
