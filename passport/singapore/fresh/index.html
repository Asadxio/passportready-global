Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Log in to MyICA using Singpass, complete online application form, and upload passport photo meeting ICA specifications. [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Pay the SGD 70 application fee online via PayNow, credit/debit card, or internet banking. [Official Source ↗]
  3. Step 3: Book collection appointment online upon receiving email/SMS notification that passport is ready. [Official Source ↗]
  4. Step 4: Collect passport in person at selected SingPost post office, or at ICA Building via automated iCollect biometric kiosks. [Official Source ↗]

Required Documents Checklist

Gather all original records and photocopies before visiting an acceptance facility or consular mission.

Digital Passport Photograph REQUIRED

Taken within last 3 months, plain white background, matt or semi-matt finish, even lighting without shadows or glasses glare.

National Registration Identity Card (NRIC) REQUIRED

Mandatory for citizens aged 15 and above upon collection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Fresh Passport in Singapore?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA), Ministry of Home Affairs via their official portal at https://www.ica.gov.sg/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Singapore Fresh Passport?

Primary requirements include: Digital Passport Photograph, National Registration Identity Card (NRIC), plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
