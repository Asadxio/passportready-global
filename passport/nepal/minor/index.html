Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Parent fills out pre-enrollment for minor using Minor ID Certificate (Nabalak Parichayapatra). [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Child and parent appear in person at DAO or DoP with original birth certificate, Nabalak Parichayapatra, parents citizenship, and marriage registration certificate. [Official Source ↗]

Required Documents Checklist

Gather all original records and photocopies before visiting an acceptance facility or consular mission.

Minor Identity Certificate (Nabalak Parichayapatra) REQUIRED

Issued by District Administration Office.

Parents Citizenship Certificates & Marriage Certificate REQUIRED

Both parents Nagarikta and formal civil marriage registration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Minor & Child Passport in Nepal?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Department of Passports (राहदानी विभाग), Ministry of Foreign Affairs via their official portal at https://nepalpassport.gov.np/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Nepal Minor & Child Passport?

Primary requirements include: Minor Identity Certificate (Nabalak Parichayapatra), Parents Citizenship Certificates & Marriage Certificate, plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
