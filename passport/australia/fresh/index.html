Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Complete application form online at AusPassport portal, then print high-resolution copy. [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Have guarantor fill section 11 and sign back of 1 photo in black ink. [Official Source ↗]
  3. Step 3: Gather original proof of citizenship and identity documents from Combination 1 or 2. [Official Source ↗]
  4. Step 4: Lodge in person at participating Australia Post post office. [Official Source ↗]

Required Documents Checklist

Gather all original records and photocopies before visiting an acceptance facility or consular mission.

Proof of Australian Citizenship REQUIRED

Full Australian birth certificate (issued by state/territory BDM) or Australian citizenship certificate.

Original Photo ID and Address Evidence REQUIRED

Current Australian driver licence, Proof of Age card, or Medicare card + bank statement.

Biometric Photo Specifications

Dimensions: 35 mm to 40 mm wide x 45 mm to 50 mm high
Head Proportions: 70%–80% of frame
Background: Plain white or light grey background
Eyeglasses: Prohibited (no reflections)
Recency: Taken within last 6 months

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Fresh Passport in Australia?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Australian Passport Office · Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade via their official portal at https://www.passports.gov.au/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Australia Fresh Passport?

Primary requirements include: Proof of Australian Citizenship, Original Photo ID and Address Evidence, plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
