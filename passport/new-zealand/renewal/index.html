Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Log in to passports.govt.nz, enter previous passport number, and upload new digital photo. [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Pay official fee (Standard NZD 247 or Urgent NZD 494) online; courier delivery is an additional cost. [Official Source ↗]

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Passport Renewal in New Zealand?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Te Tari Taiwhenua · Department of Internal Affairs via their official portal at https://www.passports.govt.nz/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for New Zealand Passport Renewal?

Primary requirements include: Previous New Zealand Passport, plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
