Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Apply in person at the Egyptian mission; the applicant (or a parent for a child) must attend. [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Submit the documents and copies and show originals for inspection; additional documents may be requested depending on the case. [Official Source ↗]
  3. Step 3: Keep the application receipt; you are notified when the application arrives and must hand in the original receipt (and expired passport, if any) at collection. [Official Source ↗]

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Fresh Passport in Egypt?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Passports, Emigration and Nationality Administration (Ministry of Interior) / Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Consular Affairs (overseas) via their official portal at https://moi.gov.eg/passportsen_fr or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Egypt Fresh Passport?

Primary requirements include: Computerized (digitized) Egyptian birth certificate, Passports of both parents, Computerized Egyptian marriage record of the parents (with the child's entry), plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
