Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Complete online pre-application (pré-demande) on passeport.ants.gouv.fr. [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Purchase fiscal stamp (timbre fiscal) online. [Official Source ↗]
  3. Step 3: Book in-person appointment at an equipped Mairie or Consulate. [Official Source ↗]
  4. Step 4: Submit original supporting documents, pay stamp, and record fingerprints (for applicants aged 12+). [Official Source ↗]
  5. Step 5: Collect passport in person within 3 months of SMS receipt. [Official Source ↗]

Required Documents Checklist

Gather all original records and photocopies before visiting an acceptance facility or consular mission.

French National Identity Card (CNI) or Birth Certificate (Acte de naissance) REQUIRED

Valid CNI proves identity and nationality. If no valid CNI, full birth certificate (acte de naissance copie intégrale) issued within last 3 months is required if birthplace not connected to COMEDEC.

Proof of Address (Justificatif de domicile) REQUIRED

Less than 1 year old (utility bill, tax notice, rent receipt).

Biometric Passport Photo (35 x 45 mm) REQUIRED

Taken within last 6 months; background must be light grey or light blue; PURE WHITE BACKGROUND IS STRICTLY REJECTED in France.

Biometric Photo Specifications

Dimensions: 35 mm wide x 45 mm high
Head Proportions: 70%–80% of frame
Background: Light grey or light blue; WHITE BACKGROUND IS STRICTLY FORBIDDEN IN FRANCE
Eyeglasses: Prohibited (no reflections)
Recency: Taken within last 6 months

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Fresh Passport in France?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Ministère de l'Intérieur · Agence Nationale des Titres Sécurisés (ANTS) via their official portal at https://passeport.ants.gouv.fr/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for France Fresh Passport?

Primary requirements include: French National Identity Card (CNI) or Birth Certificate (Acte de naissance), Proof of Address (Justificatif de domicile), Biometric Passport Photo (35 x 45 mm), plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
