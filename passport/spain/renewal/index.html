Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Passport Renewal in Spain?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Dirección General de la Policía (Policía Nacional, Ministerio del Interior) & MAEC via their official portal at https://www.dgp.mir.es/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Spain Passport Renewal?

Primary requirements include: Existing Passport Booklet & Valid DNI, plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
