Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Passport Renewal in United Arab Emirates?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP) via their official portal at https://icp.gov.ae/en/service/issue-passport/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for United Arab Emirates Passport Renewal?

Primary requirements include: Previous Emirati Passport, plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
