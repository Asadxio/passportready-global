Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Log into Passport Seva, choose 'Re-issue of Passport'. [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Provide previous passport details and reason for re-issue (Validity Expired / Exhaustion of Pages / Change in Personal Particulars). [Official Source ↗]
  3. Step 3: Pay fee, book slot, and bring old original passport for physical cancellation. [Official Source ↗]

Required Documents Checklist

Gather all original records and photocopies before visiting an acceptance facility or consular mission.

Original Existing / Expired Passport REQUIRED

Must bring old passport booklet; will be cancelled and returned with the new booklet.

Proof of Present Address REQUIRED

Required even if address is unchanged from the old passport.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Passport Renewal in India?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Passport Seva · Consular, Passport & Visa Division, Ministry of External Affairs via their official portal at https://www.passportindia.gov.in/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for India Passport Renewal?

Primary requirements include: Original Existing / Expired Passport, Proof of Present Address, plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
