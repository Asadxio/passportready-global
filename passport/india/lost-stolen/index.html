Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Immediately lodge an FIR or Missing Report at the nearest police station or state police online portal. [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Submit online application for Re-issue under 'Lost / Stolen' category. [Official Source ↗]
  3. Step 3: Attach signed Annexure F explaining circumstances of loss. [Official Source ↗]

Required Documents Checklist

Gather all original records and photocopies before visiting an acceptance facility or consular mission.

Police Report / FIR Copy (or official Lost Report Certificate) REQUIRED

Must specify passport number, date, and place of loss.

Self-Declaration on Plain Paper (Annexure F) REQUIRED

Specifying how and when the passport was lost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Lost Passport Replacement in India?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Passport Seva · Consular, Passport & Visa Division, Ministry of External Affairs via their official portal at https://www.passportindia.gov.in/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for India Lost Passport Replacement?

Primary requirements include: Police Report / FIR Copy (or official Lost Report Certificate), Self-Declaration on Plain Paper (Annexure F), plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
