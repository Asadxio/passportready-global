Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Apply online at GOV.UK (or pick up a paper form from Post Office). [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Get a digital photo code or upload a digital photo that meets HMPO criteria. [Official Source ↗]
  3. Step 3: Provide details of a countersignatory / identity referee (must know applicant for 2+ years, British/Irish passport holder in recognized profession). [Official Source ↗]
  4. Step 4: Post original supporting documents to the HMPO document handling address provided. [Official Source ↗]

Required Documents Checklist

Gather all original records and photocopies before visiting an acceptance facility or consular mission.

Full Birth Certificate or Adoption Certificate REQUIRED

Must show parents' details. Short birth certificates (without parents' names) are not accepted.

Parents' Birth Certificates / Marriage Certificate (if born in UK after 1 Jan 1983) CONDITIONAL

Required to prove British citizenship was transmitted through parents at birth.

Biometric Photo Specifications

Dimensions: 45 mm high x 35 mm wide
Head Proportions: 70%–80% of frame
Background: Light grey or cream background (not pure white)
Eyeglasses: Prohibited (no reflections)
Recency: Taken within last 6 months

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Fresh Passport in United Kingdom?

All official applications must be submitted directly through HM Passport Office · Home Office via their official portal at https://www.gov.uk/browse/abroad/passports or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for United Kingdom Fresh Passport?

Primary requirements include: Full Birth Certificate or Adoption Certificate, Parents' Birth Certificates / Marriage Certificate (if born in UK after 1 Jan 1983), plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
