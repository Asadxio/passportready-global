Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Pay the passport issuance fee through SADAD electronic government payment system. [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Log into the Absher portal (absher.sa) using your national ID credentials. [Official Source ↗]
  3. Step 3: Navigate to My Services -> Passport Services -> Issue Saudi Passport. [Official Source ↗]
  4. Step 4: Confirm applicant details and select validity period (5-year validity; or 10-year validity option available exclusively to applicants aged 21 and older). [Official Source ↗]
  5. Step 5: Select delivery method via Saudi Post (SPL) to registered national address. [Official Source ↗]

Required Documents Checklist

Gather all original records and photocopies before visiting an acceptance facility or consular mission.

Valid Saudi National Identity Card (Hawiyya Wataniyya) REQUIRED

Must be active on Absher. Minors aged 10-15 must have their biometric record registered with Civil Affairs (Al-Ahwal Al-Madaniyya).

Registered National Address (Al-Unwan Al-Watani) REQUIRED

Active registration in SPL Saudi Post address system.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Fresh Passport in Saudi Arabia?

All official applications must be submitted directly through General Directorate of Passports (Al-Jawazat) · Ministry of Interior via their official portal at https://www.absher.sa/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Saudi Arabia Fresh Passport?

Primary requirements include: Valid Saudi National Identity Card (Hawiyya Wataniyya), Registered National Address (Al-Unwan Al-Watani), plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
