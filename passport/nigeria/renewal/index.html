Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Passport Renewal in Nigeria?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS) · Federal Ministry of Interior via their official portal at https://passport.immigration.gov.ng/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Nigeria Passport Renewal?

Primary requirements include: Previous Nigerian Passport Booklet, plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
