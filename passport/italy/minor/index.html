Required Documents Checklist

Gather all original records and photocopies before visiting an acceptance facility or consular mission.

Parental Consent Declaration (Atto di assenso dei genitori) REQUIRED

Signed by both parents. If one parent refuses, an authorization order from the Guardianship Judge (Giudice Tutelare) is legally required.

Biometric Photo Specifications

Dimensions: 35mm x 40mm
Head Proportions: 70%–80% of frame
Background: White background without shadows or reflection
Eyeglasses: Prohibited (no reflections)
Recency: Taken within last 6 months

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Minor & Child Passport in Italy?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Polizia di Stato (Questura) · Ministero dell'Interno & MAECI via their official portal at https://passaportonline.poliziadistato.it/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Italy Minor & Child Passport?

Primary requirements include: Parental Consent Declaration (Atto di assenso dei genitori), plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
