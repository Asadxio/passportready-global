Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Book appointment on Agenda Passaporto portal (passaportonline.poliziadistato.it) using SPID or CIE digital identity credentials. [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Purchase official administrative tax stamp (Contrassegno telematico) of €73.50 at a tobacconist (rivendita di valori bollati / tabaccaio). [Official Source ↗]
  3. Step 3: Pay €42.50 booklet fee via pre-printed postal bulletin (bollettino postale c/c 67428014 payable to Ministero dell'Economia e delle Finanze). [Official Source ↗]
  4. Step 4: Attend appointment at Questura or Commissariato with 2 photos, payment receipts, identity card, and submit digital fingerprints. [Official Source ↗]
  5. Step 5: Collect electronic biometric passport in person or via Poste Italiane home delivery service (€9.55). [Official Source ↗]

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Fresh Passport in Italy?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Polizia di Stato (Questura) · Ministero dell'Interno & MAECI via their official portal at https://passaportonline.poliziadistato.it/ or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Italy Fresh Passport?

Primary requirements include: Valid Italian Identity Document (Carta d'Identità), Administrative Revenue Stamp (Contrassegno telematico da €73,50), Postal Payment Receipt (Ricevuta di versamento di €42,50 su c/c 67428014), plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
