Step-by-Step Application Procedure

  1. Step 1: Pay passport fee via National Bank of Pakistan (NBP), 1Link, or Passport Fee Asaan app. [Official Source ↗]
  2. Step 2: Visit Regional Passport Office or Executive Passport Center in person with original CNIC/SNIC. [Official Source ↗]
  3. Step 3: Undergo token generation, live photo capture, biometric fingerprint scanning, data entry, and interview. [Official Source ↗]
  4. Step 4: Collect passport upon SMS notification or receive via courier. [Official Source ↗]

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I officially apply for a Fresh Passport in Pakistan?

All official applications must be submitted directly through Directorate General of Immigration & Passports · Ministry of Interior via their official portal at https://dgip.gov.pk/passport/ordinary-passport.php or designated acceptance centers / embassies. PassportReady is an independent preparation guide and does not accept applications.

What are the primary documents required for Pakistan Fresh Passport?

Primary requirements include: Original CNIC / Smart National Identity Card (SNIC), Bank Challan Receipt / e-Payment Confirmation, plus compliant biometric photos and statutory fee payment.
