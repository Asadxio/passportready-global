## 1. Product Vision & Privacy-by-Design Safeguards

Applying for an international passport is frequently delayed or rejected due to minor, preventable discrepancies: inverted surnames and given names, missing secondary proofs, insufficient blank pages, or failing the international 6-month validity rule.

PassportReady organizes the checks that matter before you apply:
- **Zero Identity Retention (Privacy-by-Design):** No passport numbers, national identity numbers, exact residential addresses, or scanned identity documents are collected, transmitted, or retained.
- **100% Client-Side Processing:** All readiness evaluations, fee calculations, mismatch analysis, and checklist generation run exclusively inside the user's web browser sandbox.
- **Zero Third-Party Telemetry & Trackers:** Zero analytics trackers, zero advertising pixels, zero third-party font/script loaders, and zero cookies.
- **Strict Content Security Policy (CSP):** Enforced via meta headers forbidding remote script injection, plugins (`object-src 'none'`), and unapproved network calls.
- **Strict Hierarchy of Official Sources:** Requirements and links are grounded exclusively in official government agencies, national immigration directorates, and ICAO Doc 9303 standards.
- **Transparent Evidence Levels:** Geographic directory presence (249 jurisdictions) is strictly separated from verified procedural coverage (27 fully audited rule packs).

## 3. Core Features & Capabilities

- **10-Step Interactive Readiness Checker:**
  - Issuing country and domestic vs. consular application paths.
  - Service selection: First-time passport, renewal/reissue, lost/stolen, damaged, name correction, child/minor, expedited/Tatkaal, emergency travel certificates.
  - Age-tailored thresholds: Infant (under 5/12), child/minor (under 16/18), adult, senior citizen.
  - Statutory concession categories (e.g., Malaysia OKU disability, Hajj pilgrimage, JIM staff; Mexico Art. 20 LFD 50% discount and 30% emergency surcharge; Indonesia under-17 marriage exception).
  - Multi-dimensional evidence scoring (Base Service, Processing Urgency, Application Channel) with strict score capping.
  - Dynamic checklist with interactive progress checkboxes and direct verified government portal link.
- **Interactive Pre-Flight Tools:**
  - **6-Month Passport Expiry Calculator:** Evaluates remaining validity against travel dates across international aviation thresholds (Schengen 3-month rule vs strict Asian/Middle Eastern 6-month rule).
  - **Name & DOB Discrepancy Analyzer:** Identifies name inversions (given name vs. family name), initials, and birth date discrepancies between civil records and secondary proofs.
- **Internationalization (i18n) & RTL Layout:**
  - Complete, 100% key-parity translations (132 keys) across **6 languages**:
    - **English (`en`)**
    - **Hindi (`hi` — हिन्दी)**
    - **Urdu (`ur` — اردو)** — *Full Right-to-Left (RTL) Layout*
    - **French (`fr` — Français)**
    - **German (`de` — Deutsch)**
    - **Arabic (`ar` — العربية)** — *Full Right-to-Left (RTL) Layout*
- **Comprehensive Educational Guides:**
  - In-depth articles covering First-Time Passports, Renewals, Lost/Stolen Protocols, Legal Name Changes, Minor Protection Safeguards, 6-Month Expiry Rule, and ICAO 9303 Photo Specifications.
- **Printable & PDF-Ready Checklist:**
  - Clean `@media print` stylesheet with dedicated print layout and official disclaimer watermark.
