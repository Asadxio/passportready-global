    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    Clear expectations are part of a better application experience.

    Can PassportReady guarantee my passport will be approved?

    No. Only the relevant government authority can determine eligibility and decide an application. This tool helps organize preparation and flag issues worth checking.

    Does every country accept the same documents?

    No. Required documents, alternatives, photo specifications, forms, fees and procedures vary by issuing country, application type and individual circumstances.

    What if my name is spelled differently on two documents?

    Do not assume that a mismatch is harmless or that one particular correction is always required. Compare the records, identify the source of the discrepancy, and consult the issuing authority's official guidance for the applicable case.

    Can I use this for children, older adults or urgent applications?

    The checker can help identify the right preparation questions. Minor applications, guardianship situations and expedited services often have additional conditions, which must be verified through the official authority.

    Is this an official government service?

    No. PassportReady is an independent concept and is not affiliated with any government. Official sources should be used for final requirements, payments, appointments and application submission.
